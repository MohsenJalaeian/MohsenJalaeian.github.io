Selected Research Projects

Learning-Based Modelling and Control of Tracked Mobile Robots

Development of data-driven and physics-informed models for velocity prediction, slip estimation, and robust motion control of tracked skid-steering robots using ROS-2 and Nav2 experiments, application to Nonlinear Model Predictive Control (NMPC).

Interpretable and Sample-Efficient Reinforcement Learning

Design of fuzzy reinforcement-learning architectures, eligibility-trace mechanisms, structured experience replay, and theoretically grounded learning algorithms for continuous control.

Safe Reinforcement Learning for Nonlinear Optimal Control

Integration of actor-critic learning, adaptive experience replay, and safety-aware control formulations for uncertain nonlinear systems and constrained decision-making.

Reinforcement Learning-Model Predictive Control Integration

Systematic analysis and methodological development of RL-MPC architectures, with emphasis on linear and linearized models, constraints, robustness, sample efficiency, and closed-loop guarantees.

Autonomous Navigation and Cooperative Mobile Robotics

ROS 2/Nav2-based navigation, learning-enhanced motion prediction, autonomous mobile robotics, and cooperative multi-robot control using model predictive path integral (MPPI) methods.

Dynamic and Non-Stationary Optimization

Development of event-triggered and population-based optimization methods for dynamic environments, engineering optimization, and real-time adaptation.
